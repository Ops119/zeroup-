Private, gasless payments for AI agents.

Zeroup is a non-custodial smart wallet that pays x402 invoices from a shielded balance. Every payment comes from a fresh one-time account, and every fee is sponsored. Merchants get paid, but nobody can trace the payment back to your wallet.

The problem

x402 payments put your whole spending history on display.

x402 lets agents pay for APIs over plain HTTP. But each payment shows the payer's address, the amount and the time on chain, so the merchant, the facilitator and every chain watcher can link all of an agent's calls together.

How it works

Deposit once. Pay privately, again and again.

Your money sits in a shielded pool. Each payment is made from a fresh account funded out of that pool with a zero-knowledge proof, and a sponsor pays all the gas.

01 · DEPOSIT

Shield your USDC

Your wallet signs a gasless EIP-3009 authorization. The sponsor submits it and your USDC enters the pool as a private note.

02 · REQUEST

Agent hits a paywall

An API answers HTTP 402 with its price. Zeroup checks your spending policy: per-payment cap, daily budget and allowed hosts.

03 · PROVE

Fund a one-time account

A Groth16 proof spends a note without revealing which one. The pool pays exactly the price into a brand-new account.

04 · PAY

Settle via x402

The one-time account signs the payment, the facilitator settles it and pays the gas, and the API returns your data.

Features

Everything an agent wallet needs, private by default.

Built on open standards (x402, EIP-3009, Groth16, Poseidon, ERC-5564), with Zeroup's own engineering focused on the private payment path.

Shielded balance

A Poseidon Merkle-tree pool with Groth16 proofs. Spend part of a note and the change is re-committed privately.

One-time payers

A fresh account derived for every invoice. Merchants see an address with no past and no future.

Zero gas

Deposits, withdrawals and payments are all sponsored. Users never need ETH; fees are taken in USDC, bound into the proof.

x402 facilitator

A built-in /verify, /settle and /supported service for the x402 v2 exact scheme, plus a one-line paywall for Express APIs.

Viewing keys

Share read-only access with an accountant or auditor. They see the full history; they can never move funds.

Compliance built in

Screened deposits, and blocked addresses are refused by the pool, the sponsor and the facilitator. Anyone can always exit publicly.

Spend policy

Per-payment limits, rolling daily budgets and host allowlists, checked before any money leaves the pool.

Seed recovery

One 24-word phrase restores every note and payment. Encrypted memos on chain let the wallet rebuild itself.

Reclaim & exit

Unsettled payments flow back into the pool automatically after expiry, and you can withdraw to any public address.

For developers

Two lines to pay. Two lines to get paid.

Agents swap fetch for a private version that answers 402s automatically. APIs wrap a route with the paywall and point it at the Zeroup facilitator.

agent.ts · pay privately

const wallet = await ZeroupWallet.open({ keys, deployment, ... });
const privateFetch = createPrivateFetch(wallet);

// 402 → prove → one-time payer → settle → data
const { response, payment } =
  await privateFetch("https://api.example.com/report");

server.ts · charge per call

app.get("/report", paid(
  { facilitatorUrl, network: "eip155:8453",
    asset: USDC, payTo },
  { price: "50000", // $0.05
    description: "Research report",
    handler: (req) => buildReport(req) },
));

Roadmap

Where Zeroup is today.

The EVM payment path works end to end on a local chain and is set up for Base Sepolia. Next comes Solana, smart accounts and the road to mainnet.

Phase 0–1 built

Private agent wallet on EVM.

  • Shielded pool + Groth16 circuit
  • Gas sponsor & x402 facilitator
  • Wallet SDK, CLI, dashboard
  • Viewing keys & screening
  • 22 tests incl. adversarial cases

Phase 2 next

Both chains, better accounts.

  • Solana: Kora fee payer, Squads/Swig
  • ERC-4337 / EIP-7702 + passkeys
  • Association-set proofs
  • Network privacy relay (Tor / Nym)

Phase 3 later

Mainnet and scale.

  • Multi-party trusted-setup ceremony
  • Independent audits + bug bounty
  • Base & Solana mainnet
  • Cross-chain privacy
⚠
Testnet only.

Zeroup is unaudited, and its zero-knowledge keys come from a development setup. Don't use it with real funds until the ceremony and audits are done.
